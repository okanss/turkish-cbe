# Response to Reviewer 2 – Comment 5 (Explicit Stepwise Reasoning Follow-Up)

We thank the reviewer for this constructive suggestion. We have now executed the targeted structured stepwise-reasoning follow-up on the matched P0/P3/EN subset (2,025 prompt identities across 149 latent orders, all nine relation families, `native_shared` name control, and $n \in \{4, 16, 64\}$) using the exact same `Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct` Q4\_K\_M weights (Ollama digest `7cdf5a01`) under greedy decoding (`temperature = 0`, `top_k = 1`, `seed = 42`, `num_ctx = 8192`) with an expanded 4,096-token output budget (`src/inference/ollama_reasoning_runner.py`, `scripts/analyze_followup.py`, full records in `results/followup/qwen2-5-14b_structured.jsonl` and `results/followup/followup_summary.json`).

**Key findings added to Section 7.8 (Table 14) and Section 8.3:**

- **The P3 deficit persists under explicit stepwise reasoning:** Under the all-planned estimand, the P3-minus-canonical contrast narrows in magnitude from $-0.403$ (95\% CI $[-0.452, -0.355]$) in the direct arm to $-0.204$ ($[-0.245, -0.164]$) in the structured arm (EN P0: $73.2\%$, TR P0: $60.4\%$, TR P3: $40.0\%$), while the Turkish–English gap widens slightly from $-0.076$ ($[-0.130, -0.014]$) to $-0.127$ ($[-0.175, -0.076]$).
- **Invalid-response audit and jointly valid confirmation:** Strict-parser invalid responses rose from $1/2025$ ($0.05\%$) in the direct arm to $515/2025$ ($25.43\%$) in the structured arm: $139$ output-length truncations at $4,096$ tokens ($123$ of which occurred at $n = 64$), $338$ final-line format-marker deviations (typically prefixing the final line with a numbered step such as `4. FINAL: YES`), and $38$ duplicate `FINAL:` markers (zero transport errors). Because invalid rates are comparable across conditions ($24.9\%$ EN P0, $27.0\%$ TR P0, $24.4\%$ TR P3), the P3 collapse is not an artifact of differential formatting failures: on valid responses alone, accuracy is $97.4\%$ for EN P0 ($494/507$), $82.8\%$ for TR P0 ($408/493$), and $52.9\%$ for TR P3 ($270/510$), yielding a jointly valid P3 deficit of $\widehat{\Delta}_{\mathrm{P3-P0}} = -0.289$ ($391/675$ pairs, $57.9\%$ coverage) and $\widehat{\Delta}_{\mathrm{TR-EN}} = -0.131$ ($375/675$ pairs, $55.6\%$ coverage).
- **Complexity decline persists:** On valid responses, accuracy decreases monotonically with entity count from $n = 4$ to $n = 16$ to $n = 64$ across EN P0 ($100.0\% \to 97.9\% \to 95.1\%$), TR P0 ($91.7\% \to 82.6\% \to 75.2\%$), and TR P3 ($66.9\% \to 50.0\% \to 43.9\%$). Under the all-planned estimand, TR P3 likewise declines monotonically ($44.9\% \to 41.8\% \to 33.3\%$), whereas EN P0 ($59.6\% \to 83.1\% \to 76.9\%$) and TR P0 ($58.7\% \to 67.6\% \to 55.1\%$) dip at $n = 4$ due to strict marker-format deviations ($36.0\%$–$40.4\%$ invalid at $n = 4$ with zero truncations) before declining from $n = 16$ to $n = 64$ as truncations grow.
