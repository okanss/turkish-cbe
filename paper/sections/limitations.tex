\section{Limitations}

\paragraph{Empirical availability.}
Model evidence is limited to the two exploratory pilot runs of Section~\ref{sec:pilot} (pilot split, one
relation family, one name control, $n \le 32$) and the supplemental quantized local sweep of
Section~\ref{sec:p1-local} (pilot split, nine relation families, $n \le 128$, Q4\_K\_M weights that are
not precision-comparable to registered snapshots); the sampled-stimulus review of
Section~\ref{sec:review} backs grammaticality and meaning preservation on this population, but it is
sampled review with contested naturalness, not exhaustive certification. No
frozen-test inference or adapter evaluation outcome is available, and the only additional model family is
quantized and supplemental; the executed training runs are smoke-scale backend validation, not adaptation
evidence. Dataset counts
and corpus/tokenizer measurements are real, but neither they nor the pilot support confirmatory claims
about language gaps or intervention efficacy. The draft is not a completed empirical evaluation or an
externally preregistered study.

\paragraph{Linguistic coverage.}
The renderer covers a closed grammar and a finite relation inventory. Shared generation/parsing code can
conceal a common mistake. The completed human review (Section~\ref{sec:review}) certifies equivalence
unanimously but is sampled, and its naturalness dimension shows low inter-reviewer agreement; it is not an
exhaustive naturalness proof. Turkish morphology and word
order are richer than the implemented templates, and P4/P5 do not identify a pure morphology manipulation.
Minimal-pair results in prior literature cannot validate our stimuli automatically.

\paragraph{Task and sample scope.}
Strict total orders exclude partial orders, contradictory premises, ties, and open-world uncertainty.
Renamed orders are structurally isomorphic. Multiple queries, names, relations, and presentations share each
latent source; millions of records do not supply millions of independent samples. Exact latent split
separation does not establish absence of model pretraining contamination.

\paragraph{Frequency and tokenization proxies.}
BOUN has finite genre/sense coverage. Surface $n$-gram and lemma opportunities differ, exact canonical zeros
need not reflect general language usage, and lemma counts are not relation-sense counts. Tokenizer
measurements cover only the six registered configurations, and configurations sharing a tokenizer are not
independent samples. Isolated-string measurements exclude chat overhead and cannot establish input
truncation or billed usage. Correlations with model performance cannot be estimated without actual
predictions.

\paragraph{Inference and missingness.}
Context overflow, wrong-language answers, truncation, and provider nondeterminism may be systematically
associated with difficult items. Conditional CBE statistics require coverage and all-planned counterparts.
Cluster bootstrap intervals depend on sufficient independent latent orders; Wilson intervals alone do not
account for repeated derived rows. The declared Holm families do not provide study-wide simultaneous error
control.

\paragraph{Training feasibility and transfer.}
The training backend and its declared dependency pins are now validated by an executed smoke-scale run of
all five arms on rented CUDA hardware (Section~\ref{sec:pilot}); the local M1/16-GiB environment remains
unsuitable for training. No adapter has been evaluated, so no adaptation effect of any direction is
established. Equal steps do not equalize examples, tokens, GPU time, or update budgets. Held-out relation
terms can share context vocabulary, limiting lexical-transfer interpretations.
