\section{Discussion}

\subsection{From translation control to linguistic construct validity}

Matching a latent order controls the intended proposition, but it does not ensure that two sentences are
equally natural, equally familiar, or equally easy to parse. A Turkish postverbal constituent may preserve
truth while changing discourse preference. A morphological alternative may alter word length and lexical
realization at the same time. These distinctions connect Turkish-CBE to linguistic evaluation work
\citep{ismayilzada2025morphology, basar2025turblimp, basar2026morphologyaware} without making grammatical
acceptability interchangeable with logical accuracy. Human adjudication should therefore evaluate more than
whether a parser recovers the expected relation.

The zero exact surface counts illustrate a related measurement issue. An inflected, multiword canonical
predicate can be absent under exact matching even when its constituent lemma is frequent. The difference is
substantively meaningful for the feature definition, but neither variable directly measures exposure in a
proprietary model's training corpus. Lemma ambiguity and corpus domain must remain part of the
interpretation. Tokenizer studies likewise motivate explicit measurement \citep{rust2021tokenizer,
ahia2023languages, petrov2023tokenizers}, not a presumption that subword length is the unique cause of an
error.

\subsection{Correct consistency versus stable error}

The paired decomposition supports questions that a marginal accuracy table cannot answer. A small average
difference between canonical and equivalent prompts can coexist with large, offsetting correct-to-wrong and
wrong-to-correct flows. Conversely, agreement can improve because a model repeats a wrong label.
Equations~\eqref{eq:identities}--\eqref{eq:strict} specify the evidence needed to distinguish these cases:
shared-valid $R/D/U$, all-planned accuracy and strict $R$, invalid rates, and coverage. Comparisons that
condition on different subsets can otherwise make a selectively valid model appear more robust.

This interpretation also limits the role of chance correction. The pooled-marginal baseline discounts a
particular independent-answer bias reference. It is not a causal model of failure, nor does a high
coefficient show that the same inferential algorithm was applied across forms. Correctness and invariance
remain behavioral properties. Explanation text adds another observation channel but not automatic access to
the model's internal computation \citep{turpin2023unfaithful}.

\subsection{Complexity and intervention claims}

The endpoint shortcut makes a strong endpoint score ambiguous as evidence of transitive reasoning.
Internal/distance-controlled questions reduce one specific shortcut while retaining a highly structured task
family; they do not eliminate every efficient alternative strategy. Changes with $n$ may reflect retrieval,
distraction, context length, or the distribution of queried distances. Any future complexity claim must
therefore name its query stratum and input/presentation condition.

Reasoning prompting may change token budgets and final-answer formatting as well as intermediate generation.
Provider reasoning effort is a different intervention from an explicit verbal instruction. Similarly, SFT or
GRPO may strengthen a learned vocabulary mapping without producing broad transfer. Prior work on prompting,
aggregation, and adaptation \citep{wei2022cot, wang2023selfconsistency, hu2021lora, shao2024deepseekmath}
supplies methodological options, not evidence for an effect in this benchmark. A finite improvement can
extend successful coverage without establishing competence throughout the family.

\subsection{What the pilot evidence adds}

The pilot runs convert three of this article's methodological arguments from anticipation into observation.
First, the degenerate-responder case is real: Qwen2.5-0.5B attains perfect raw invariance by answering
constantly per language, and only the joint presentation of $R$, $D$, $U$, and the undefined
$\kappa_{\mathrm{CBE}}$ distinguishes this from robustness. Any evaluation protocol that reports agreement
without the decomposition would have certified this model. Second, the two failure channels that the
decomposition separates both occur in a frontier model: moving gpt-4o from English to Turkish raises
presentation dependence $U$ while leaving shared error $D$ nearly unchanged, whereas the P3 construction
collapses agreement to chance --- different mechanisms that a marginal accuracy table cannot distinguish.
Third, the power analysis behind the frozen-test sample sizes is vindicated in both directions:
eight-pair strata detect nothing for the consistently negative Turkish--English contrast, while the P3
effect is large enough to survive Holm correction even at pilot scale. The lexical join adds a fourth
observation: the direction asymmetry of Table~\ref{tab:direction} aligns with measured frequency and
tokenization asymmetries, yet the single-relation cohort leaves every association formally unidentified ---
the specifications return recorded refusals, not coefficients, exactly as the protocol requires.

The pilot equally illustrates the interpretive discipline the framework imposes. The P3 deficit is measured
on precisely the condition whose naturalness most depends on human review; before that review, a model
failure and a stimulus artifact were observationally equivalent, and the completed review
(Section~\ref{sec:review}) now separates them in part --- grammaticality and meaning preservation are
certified, naturalness stays contested. The endpoint
$>$ internal $>$ distance-controlled ordering is consistent with the endpoint-shortcut analysis above, and
the below-baseline accuracy at large $d$ shows a systematic directional error, but both observations cover
one relation family on shared latent orders. The supplemental quantized sweep extends the same structure
to nine relation families and four scales --- and its sharpest result, the $-38$-point query-matched P3
collapse at 14B, lands on the same naturalness-contested condition, so the residual naturalness ambiguity
scales with it. These are reasons to run the confirmatory design, not conclusions from it.

\subsection{Status of the scientific claims}

The supported conclusions are restricted to the constructed design, recorded preprocessing measurements,
and the exploratory pilot runs of Section~\ref{sec:pilot}. The framework makes comparison units, linguistic
transformations, missingness, and inferential assumptions explicit. It also exposes gaps that must be
filled: human review, frozen-test model calls under the declared primary test families, and the reviewed
base-versus-adapter evaluation of the already-trained optional adaptation arms. Neither an expected directional hypothesis nor a
favorable narrative is substituted for missing outcomes; the pilot's suggestive Turkish--English direction
is reported with its failure to reach per-stratum significance, not promoted beyond it. This separation
allows future empirical findings, including null or adverse effects, to be incorporated without changing
the underlying definitions after observing results.
